Timmble – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Timmble is a learning-and-play app for children, designed to be set up and managed by a parent or guardian. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how — including what will be collected when optional features like accounts and subscriptions are used.

Who We Are

Timmble is operated by SafeStop (contact: abhishek.safestop@gmail.com). We are based in Bangalore, India.

What We Collect

Always (no account required)

  • Device ID — a randomly generated identifier stored on your device. Not linked to any name, email, or real-world identity.
  • Age group — selected by the parent (3–5 yrs, 6–8 yrs, or 9–12 yrs). Used only to set appropriate difficulty levels.
  • Game scores and session data — scores, correct answers, time taken, win/loss results. Used to show progress in the Parent Dashboard.
  • Warm-up results — skill-area scores (memory, attention, pattern, spatial, logic) generated from in-app activities.

When You Create an Account (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google or create an account to sync progress across devices or manage a subscription, we additionally collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and send receipts or important updates.
  • Name — as provided by your Google account, displayed in the Parent Dashboard.
  • Profile photo — as provided by your Google account (optional, display only).
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active paid plan, used to unlock premium content. Billing is handled entirely by the Google Play Store; we never see your payment card details.

Account creation is always optional. The app is fully usable without signing in.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No location data
  • No browsing history or cross-app tracking
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID)
  • No payment card details (billing is handled by Google Play)
  • No data from the child directly — all account setup is done by the parent

How We Use the Data

All data collected is used solely to:

  • Adapt game difficulty to the child's age group and performance
  • Display progress and skills overview in the Parent Dashboard
  • Track streaks and session history
  • Sync progress across devices when signed in
  • Manage subscription access to premium content
  • Send transactional emails (receipts, subscription updates) — never marketing without explicit opt-in

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share data only with:

  • Google Play — to process payments and verify subscription status.
  • Google Sign-In — only if you choose to use it for authentication.
  • AWS — our hosting provider (ap-south-1 region, India). Data is stored securely and not accessible to AWS for any purpose beyond infrastructure.

No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics platforms.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time by tapping Reset All Data in the Dashboard screen. To request complete server-side deletion of your account and all associated data, email abhishek.safestop@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request." We will action it within 30 days.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Timmble is set up and managed by a parent or guardian — not by the child. We do not collect personal information from children. The device ID is a random number not linked to any child's identity. Account creation (which involves email) is done by the parent. Parents can delete all data at any time via the app or by contacting us.

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Our servers are hosted on AWS with industry-standard access controls. We do not store payment information.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this page when our data practices change and notify signed-in users by email for material changes. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
